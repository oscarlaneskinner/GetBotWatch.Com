01Who We Are

BotWatch ("we," "us," "our") is a company based in the United States. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit this site or purchase a BotWatch license, and how we handle it.

02What We Collect

  • →Purchase information: name, email address, and license tier purchased — collected by our payment processor at checkout.
  • →Support communications: anything you send us directly, e.g. via email, for setup help or custom service requests.
  • →Basic site analytics: aggregate, non-identifying traffic data (e.g. page views), if analytics tools are enabled on this site.
  • →Anonymous crash reports: the BotWatch software itself sends a crash report (error type, stack trace, file/line numbers only) if it hits an unexpected bug, so we can fix it. This never includes your trading data, account balances, API keys, symbols, or results, and can be turned off — see the Disclaimer for details.

We do not collect your trading account credentials, brokerage API keys, or trading data — through this website or through the crash reporting described above. Any such information you configure within the BotWatch software itself stays on your own machine/environment — see the Disclaimer for details on how the software handles your own configuration.

03How We Use It

  • →To deliver your license/download and process your purchase
  • →To provide support and respond to custom service requests
  • →To send you important updates about the software (e.g. new versions, security notices)
  • →To send occasional product or promotional emails — you can unsubscribe at any time

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

05Cookies & Analytics

This site may use minimal, non-intrusive analytics to understand aggregate traffic (e.g. how many people visit a page). We do not use tracking cookies for cross-site ad targeting. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated and a cookie consent notice will be added where required by law.

06International Data Transfers

Because BotWatch is sold globally, your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where we or our service providers operate. Where required (e.g. for customers in the EU/UK), our payment processor maintains appropriate safeguards for international data transfer in compliance with GDPR.

07Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • →Access the personal data we hold about you
  • →Request correction of inaccurate data
  • →Request deletion of your data (subject to legal/tax record-keeping requirements)
  • →Opt out of marketing emails at any time
  • →Object to or restrict certain processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We'll respond within a reasonable timeframe and as required by applicable law (e.g. within 30 days under GDPR).

08Data Retention

We retain purchase records for as long as necessary to comply with tax and accounting obligations, and support communications for as long as reasonably useful for providing ongoing support. You can request earlier deletion of non-essential data at any time.

09Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold, including relying on payment processors with industry-standard security certifications (e.g. PCI DSS compliance) for all payment handling. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted online.

10Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
