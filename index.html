Why it matters

Automated trading moves faster than you can watch manually — that's exactly the problem.

A stuck position, a bad fill, a strategy that keeps trading through conditions it was never built for — these are the quiet failures that drain an account before anyone notices.

◆ FAILURE MODE

The bot keeps trading through a broken signal

Without a monitoring layer, a strategy can run for hours on bad data or a logic error before you'd ever catch it manually.

◆ FAILURE MODE

Exposure creeps up without you noticing

Position sizing drifts over time as a strategy compounds gains or losses — invisible unless something is actively tracking it against your baseline.

◆ FAILURE MODE

An API hiccup goes unnoticed for hours

A stuck connection or delayed fill can leave a bot silently exposed. BotWatch flags heartbeat and latency issues as they happen, not after.

Purchase FAQ

The questions we actually get asked, answered plainly.

What exactly do I get when I buy?

A downloadable software package: the full BotWatch dashboard, the GLD Trend Bot, documentation, and a license key. It runs on your own computer — nothing is hosted for you.

How is it delivered?

Instantly. After checkout, Lemon Squeezy emails your license key and download link to the email address you enter at checkout — usually within a minute or two.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The dashboard is point-and-click for connecting accounts, testing strategies, and monitoring. If you do want to customize the underlying bot code, most buyers do it with the help of Claude or ChatGPT rather than writing it from scratch — see the "Built to be customized" section above.

Can I use it on more than one account or bot?

Yes, across the bots and accounts you personally control under one license. If you're setting it up for someone else too — family, a friend — see the Gift Packs above or use the "Send as a Gift" option; a multi-pack's single license key works across everyone you share it with (up to that pack's install limit), but each person still runs their own copy and connects their own broker keys locally.

Does BotWatch place trades for me automatically?

The included GLD bot can place paper or live trades through your connected broker if you choose to run it that way, but BotWatch itself is a research and monitoring layer, not a managed account — you're always the one deciding what runs and when.

Can I trade crypto with BotWatch?

Yes — set the symbol to a crypto pair like BTC/USD instead of GLD (via Alpaca, the same broker connection you're already using) and the bot can run continuously, since crypto markets don't close the way stock markets do. "Can run 24/7" describes that technical capability, not a promise of profit — see the crypto trading disclaimer for the full picture.

Is my broker data safe?

Your API keys are stored in a local file on your own machine and used only to talk directly to your broker's API. We never see them, store them, or transmit them anywhere.

What's your refund policy?

All sales are final — as a digital product, the license key and full download are delivered instantly, so it can't be "returned" the way a physical product can. Review the free info, screenshots, and this FAQ before buying. Full terms are in the disclaimer.

What if I want something changed or built that isn't included?

That's what the hourly custom-work option above is for — buy a block of hours, then tell us what you need. Self-customizing the code yourself is always free and included with your license.

Is email or phone support available?

Support is email-only (hello@getbotwatch.com) — no live support (phone or chat). But the real beauty of this product is that it's built to change: because you own the full source code, most updates don't require waiting on anyone. If you have Claude Pro (or any AI coding assistant), point it at your BotWatch files and describe what you want changed — a different stop-loss, a new indicator, your own custom feature, even a unicorns-and-rainbows theme if that's genuinely what you're after — and it can typically make the edit in minutes, for a fraction of what hourly help would cost. That keeps costs down for you. Don't have Claude Pro? The free version works too — just copy the relevant file's code into the chat, describe the change, and paste the result back in. Prefer we just make the change for you directly? That's exactly what the hourly Custom Work option above is for.
